About

The art of every cup

I'm Willson Rai, a Head Barista and creative freelancer from Lalitpur, Nepal. Over seven years in the coffee and hospitality industry, I've grown from pulling my first espresso shot to leading teams, training new baristas, and building visual identities for cafés and restaurants.

Beyond the bar, I work as a creative freelancer under the BARISTA brand — designing menus, shooting culinary photography and video, and crafting social content. Coffee and creativity run through everything I make.
