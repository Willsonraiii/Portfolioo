    About Me

    The Art of Every Cup

    I'm Willson Rai, a passionate Head Barista and creative freelancer based in Lalitpur, in the Kathmandu Valley, Nepal. With over seven years in the coffee and hospitality industry, I've grown from pulling my first espresso shot to leading teams, training new baristas, and creating visual identities for cafés and restaurants across Kathmandu and Lalitpur.

    Beyond the bar, I wear the hat of a creative freelancer — designing menus, crafting social media content, and building brand identities. Coffee and creativity flow through everything I do.
